# Deep-space milestone — 2026-10-02

Atlas opens outside the Milky Way and supports Earth, the planets, Moon, Sun, named nearby stars, and eight external galaxies. The independent deep-space render pass contains 61,450 real HYG v4.1 catalogue points with positions in parsecs, apparent magnitude recomputed from absolute magnitude and camera distance, and approximate B−V colour. Selection excludes unknown-distance sentinel records. The catalogue is fixed to J2000; proper motion is not propagated.

Galaxy coordinates, mean distances, source references and standard errors are preserved in `public/data/galaxies.json`, normalized from the NED OverviewOfObject VOTable API. Destinations: Andromeda, Triangulum, Whirlpool, M81, Sombrero, M87, LMC and SMC. The Milky Way uses a galactic-centre reference. NED data: https://ned.ipac.caltech.edu/ ; API: https://ned.ipac.caltech.edu/Docs::API/ . HYG: https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database . Dataset attribution and CC BY-SA 4.0 terms: `public/data/LICENSE.txt`.

Galaxy appearance is a GPU volume integration of approximate morphology, stellar-population colours, dust attenuation, spiral arms, bulge and aggregate star-forming regions. It is not a photo, individual-star survey of other galaxies, measured 3D dust tomography, or dynamically simulated galaxy. Inclinations and position angles are approximate authoring profiles. Missing diameter estimates (M51 and LMC) use disclosed visualization fallbacks. Close-up stellar radii and temperatures are derived photometric estimates; granulation is simulated. No invented catalogue points or names are added to the star field.

The camera stores a float64 scientific origin separately from its small local offset. Foreground geometry is normalized to the current radius and uses logarithmic depth. Catalogue stars use high/low origin subtraction in parsecs; their stored GPU coordinates remain float32, so distant point precision is limited. This is not arbitrary-precision navigation everywhere. The foreground and deep-space passes have separate depth buffers, followed by bloom and ACES output. Cinematic flights interpolate logarithmic distance and deliberately exceed physical travel speeds.

Installed Microsoft Store Blender 5.2.2.0 was run through its `blender-launcher.exe` alias, in background mode. `scripts/blender-assets.py` exports a measured-oblate Saturn mesh with a UV observation-derived map to `public/models/saturn.glb`. The live renderer imports its baked geometry; reconstructed rings and sunlight remain real-time shaders. It also saves `public/models/atlas-catalogue.blend` with the 61,450 actual HYG positions as editable mesh vertices in equatorial J2000 parsecs. The Blender scene is authoring data, not a pre-rendered replacement for exploration.

Reproduce the star dataset with `node scripts/ingest-hyg.mjs` after obtaining the official HYG archive at `artifacts/hyg-v41.csv`. Reproduce the Blender exports with your installed Blender: `blender-launcher.exe --background --python scripts/blender-assets.py`.

Validation: 18 automated tests cover orbital mathematics, frame rebasing, small local offsets at large coordinates, orthogonal coordinate transforms and rejected unknown-distance records. TypeScript, ESLint and production build pass. Browser views checked: Milky Way, real-star travel and Saturn GLB. Nebula catalogues, black-hole ray tracing, cosmological large-scale structure and a complete Gaia survey remain future milestones.
